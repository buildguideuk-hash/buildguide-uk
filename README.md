# BuildGuide UK

**AI-Powered Assistant for UK Construction Tradespeople**

BuildGuide UK is a practical, on-site AI assistant designed specifically for builders, bricklayers, groundworkers, small construction firms, and other trades across the United Kingdom.

It provides clear, regulation-compliant guidance based on current **UK Building Regulations** and **NHBC Standards**.

### Key Features

- **Step-by-step Method Statements** – Detailed, practical instructions from start to finish
- **Comprehensive Risk Assessments** – Hazards, who might be harmed, control measures, and PPE requirements
- **Materials Lists** – Realistic quantities and suggested UK suppliers (Jewson, Travis Perkins, Selco, etc.)
- **Compliant Advice** – References to UK Building Regulations and NHBC Standards
- **Simple Chat Interface** – Just type your question and get structured, usable answers

### How to Use

1. Open the app
2. Type your question in the chat box, for example:
   - "How do I build foundations for a small extension?"
   - "What is the correct way to lay a brick wall with cavity?"
   - "How do I install damp proof course?"
3. Receive structured guidance including Method Statement, Risk Assessment, and Materials

### Important Disclaimer

**This is AI-generated guidance only.**  
All advice is provided for informational purposes. Users remain fully responsible for:
- Verifying compliance with the latest UK Building Regulations and NHBC Standards
- Obtaining necessary approvals from Building Control
- Ensuring work is carried out safely and legally
- Consulting qualified professionals where required

Always check current regulations before starting any work.
